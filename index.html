Siemens Immersive Design Challenge · Finalist

RoboRowdy

An autonomous robot that automates 3D print farm post-processing: removing finished parts, cleaning build plates, and restarting prints without human intervention. Designed in Siemens NX, validated with FEA and topology optimization, and presented on stage at Realize Live Americas 2026 in Detroit.
